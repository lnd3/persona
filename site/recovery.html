Recovery

Designating guardians and vouching for a recovery attempt are both just signed events — the same real window.nostr/NIP-07 flow as attesting. This page covers that half only. The other half — actually splitting your key into guardian shares and delivering them — has to happen inside a signer extension itself, never a web page, since that needs your raw private key, which NIP-07 never exposes here. See A018 for why, and what's not built yet.

1. Designate guardians

Signs one recovery_guardian claim per guardian, all sharing a fresh group ID — save that group ID, guardians and any future recovery attempt both need it.

2. Confirm as a guardian

If you've been designated as a guardian and the owner has told you (out of band — a recovery attempt isn't itself published by anyone until a guardian confirms it) which group ID and recovery nonce to use, sign your confirmation here.

Starting a recovery attempt? Generate a nonce here and give it to your guardians yourself — there's no separate "start recovery" event; the nonce is just the thing that ties everyone's confirmations to the same attempt.

3. Check recovery status

Looks up the real guardian set and real confirmations for one owner/group/nonce from real relays — the same check internal/recovery.ThresholdMet does in Go, ported directly, not re-derived.

No signer loaded? check you have one first. This page never sees your private key — only window.nostr does, inside your own extension.
